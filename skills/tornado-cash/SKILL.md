Tornado Cash is an Ethereum mixer that uses zk-SNARK proofs to break the on-chain
link between deposit and withdrawal. The pools and their primary smart contracts
are subject to OFAC sanctions (US Treasury SDN listing, August 2022). The agent
surfaces this status factually to the user; the legal decision belongs to the
user, not the wallet.
